# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want working on this project to be a safe and productive experience for everyone,
regardless of experience level, gender identity and expression, sexual orientation,
disability, physical appearance, body size, ethnicity, nationality, race, age or religion.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in reviews, issues and discussions.
- Critique code and decisions, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Keep reports reproducible: version, steps, logs.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory or sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or deliberate bad-faith reporting.

## Scope

This policy applies to all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests,
reviews and any channel used to represent the project.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/sepehrsdq/Sepehr-Meeting/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting the repository owner. Reports are reviewed confidentially.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this policy may face temporary or permanent
consequences as determined by the project owner.
